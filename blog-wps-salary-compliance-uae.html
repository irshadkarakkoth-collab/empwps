WPS Salary Compliance in the UAE: What Employers Must Know

Published 2 June 2026  ·  7 min read  ·  By Irshadkarakkoth

The UAE Wage Protection System (WPS) is a government initiative managed by MOHRE that requires all private-sector employers to pay salaries electronically through approved financial institutions. It ensures workers receive their wages on time and in full. Non-compliance carries significant financial and operational penalties.

The Four WPS Salary Statuses

Every employee's salary record in EMPWPSCTR is marked with one of four statuses:

✅ PAID

Full salary paid on time. No action required.

⚠️ PARTIAL

Salary paid but less than the contracted amount. Needs follow-up.

❌ UNPAID

No salary transferred this cycle. Urgent action required.

— NO CONTRACT

No employment contract registered in MOHRE for this employee.

Payment Deadlines

Under UAE Labour Law, salaries must be paid within 10 days of the date specified in the employment contract. For employees paid monthly, this means no later than the 10th of the following month. Employers who miss this window are flagged as non-compliant in the WPS system.

⚠️ Even a single missed or short-paid salary cycle can trigger a MOHRE alert and suspend your ability to issue new work permits.

Penalties for WPS Non-Compliance

  • Permit ban: New and renewal work permit applications are blocked until the violation is resolved.
  • Financial fines: Fines per affected employee, escalating with repeat violations.
  • Labour Court referral: Serious or persistent violations can be referred for prosecution.
  • Company blacklisting: Repeat offenders may be added to MOHRE's restricted list.

The Salary Percentage Threshold Feature

EMPWPSCTR adds a % Threshold check on top of the standard PAID / PARTIAL / UNPAID status. Before extraction you set a percentage (e.g. 80%). The % column then labels every employee:

  • >80% — paid at least 80% of contracted salary
  • <80% — paid less than 80% — requires investigation
  • 100% or >100% — full or over-payment

This lets HR teams quickly spot employees who are technically marked PARTIAL but are actually paid close to their full salary versus those with serious shortfalls.

💡 The % column calculation is: (Paid Salary ÷ Contract Salary) × 100. EMPWPSCTR detects these columns automatically from the WPS PDF.

How to Check WPS Compliance with EMPWPSCTR

  1. Switch to the WPS tab inside EMPWPSCTR.
  2. Upload your WPS salary PDF from MOHRE.
  3. Click Extract — a modal will ask for your % threshold (default 80%).
  4. Enter your threshold and click Extract in the modal.
  5. Review the table: filter by status column to isolate UNPAID or PARTIAL rows.
  6. Export to Excel for payroll team review and record-keeping.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the WPS deadline in the UAE?

Employers must pay salaries within 10 days of the contractual due date. Monthly-paid employees must receive salaries by the 10th of the following month.

What does PARTIAL mean in WPS?

PARTIAL means the employer transferred a salary amount that is less than the contracted figure registered with MOHRE. The EMPWPSCTR % column shows exactly how much less was paid.

What are the penalties for WPS non-compliance?

Penalties include suspension of work permit applications, fines per affected employee, Labour Court referral and potential company blacklisting by MOHRE.

How do I check all employees at once?

Upload your WPS salary PDF, set a % threshold when prompted, and click Extract. The entire employee list is checked in seconds — no manual checking required.
