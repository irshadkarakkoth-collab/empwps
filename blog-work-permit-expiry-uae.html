Managing UAE Work Permit Expiry: Avoid Fines and Overstays

Published 2 June 2026  ·  6 min read  ·  By Irshadkarakkoth

Keeping track of work permit expiry dates across dozens or hundreds of employees is one of the most error-prone tasks for UAE HR teams. A missed renewal means overstay fines, potential employee deportation and disruption to operations. EMPWPSCTR gives every expiry date a colour-coded status the moment you upload your MOHRE employment list PDF.

The Four Expiry Colour Codes

Each employee's Expiry Date cell is highlighted in one of four colours, both in the on-screen table and in the Excel export:

🔴 Expired

Work permit has already lapsed. Immediate action required.

🟠 Expiring <30 days

Renew urgently. Fine risk rising daily.

🟡 Expiring <90 days

Start renewal process now to avoid last-minute delays.

🟢 Valid

No action needed at this time.

⚠️ As of 2026, UAE overstay fines accrue daily. Even a single day of overstay triggers a penalty that must be settled before the employee can renew or exit.

Why Tracking Expiry Manually Fails

Most companies attempt to track expiry dates in a shared Excel sheet updated manually. This approach fails because:

  • The sheet is only as current as the last person who updated it.
  • With large workforces, individual updates are often missed or entered incorrectly.
  • There is no automated alert when a date passes from yellow to orange to red.
  • Staff turnover means institutional knowledge of who owns the tracking task is lost.

EMPWPSCTR solves this by reading directly from the official MOHRE PDF — the same source of truth MOHRE uses — so the data is always accurate to the latest issued document.

How to Find All Expiring Permits in One Step

  1. Upload your latest MOHRE employment list PDF to EMPWPSCTR.
  2. Click Extract to populate the table.
  3. Click the Expiry Date column header to sort by date ascending — expired rows rise to the top.
  4. Use the per-column filter on the Expiry Date column to enter a date range, e.g. all dates before DD/MM/YYYY.
  5. Click Excel → With Colors to export a colour-coded renewal action list for your PRO or typing centre.

💡 Run this check every time MOHRE issues an updated employment list PDF — typically after each new visa or permit transaction.

Renewal Timeline: When to Act

  • 90 days before expiry (Yellow): Collect employee documents, start medical fitness tests and Emirates ID pre-approval.
  • 30 days before expiry (Orange): Submit work permit renewal application to MOHRE. Begin visa stamping process.
  • 0 days / Expired (Red): Apply for an urgent renewal or out-of-status resolution immediately. Legal advice may be required.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fine for an expired work permit in UAE?

Overstay fines in the UAE accrue daily after the permit expiry date. Employers may also face penalties for retaining workers with expired permits. Check GDRFA and MOHRE for current rates as amounts are periodically updated.

How early should I renew a UAE work permit?

MOHRE recommends initiating renewal at least 30 days before expiry. EMPWPSCTR's yellow flag at 90 days gives your team ample time to prepare documents, medicals and Emirates ID before the 30-day deadline.

Can I sort employees by work permit expiry date?

Yes. Click the Expiry Date column header in EMPWPSCTR to sort all employee rows by date, ascending or descending.

Does the expiry colour coding appear in the Excel export?

Yes. Choose With Colors when exporting to Excel — the expiry date cells carry the same red / orange / yellow / green fill as shown on screen.
